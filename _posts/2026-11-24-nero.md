NERO is a 7-degree-of-freedom robotic arm developed by AgileX Robotics for humanoid robotics, embodied AI, robot learning and manipulation research.

Its redundant kinematic configuration enables flexible arm posture adjustment and motion planning in constrained workspaces. The arm weighs 4.8 kg, carries a payload of up to 3 kg, has a reach of 580 mm and provides a repeatability of ±0.1 mm.

NERO supports tabletop, side-mounted and inverted installation, allowing integration with mobile robot platforms, humanoid robots and dual-arm manipulation systems. It can be equipped with grippers, dexterous hands, cameras and other sensors for perception and manipulation tasks.

The arm provides ROS 2 and Python SDK interfaces for motion control and system integration. Public development resources include ROS 2 drivers, URDF models, MoveIt 2 packages and simulation tools, supporting applications in teleoperation, motion planning, mobile manipulation and robot learning.
