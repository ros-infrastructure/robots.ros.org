**Mobile Manipulator for R&D applications in outdoor environments**

RB-VOGUI+ is an autonomous mobile manipulator designed to develop R&D applications in outdoor environments, performing tasks related to agriculture or construction.
This mobile manipulator combines the general purpose RB-VOGUI platform with Universal Robots’ UR5e or UR10e robotic arms.

RB-VOGUI+ with an UR5e can carry up to 125 kg of weight on the platform and manipulate up to 5 kg with the arm, while RB-VOGUI+ with an UR10e can carry up to 112 kg of weight on the platform and manipulate up to 12,5 kg with the arm. This allows RB-VOGUI+ to perform manipulation tasks like planting, harvesting and monitoring crops, pesticides and fertilizers or to transport and place materials in construction environments.

All [Robotnik](https://robotnik.eu/) robots are modular and their architecture is based on ROS or ROS 2.

## Characteristics
### Mechanical
* Dimensions:  1.044 x 776 x 742 mm
* Weight:
    * Platform: 165 kg
    * UR5e arm: 25 kg
    * UR10e arm: 38 kg
* Payload:
    * With UR5e:
        * Platform: 125 kg
        * Arm: Up to 5 kg
    * With UR10e:
        * Platform: 112 kg
        * Arm: Up to 12,5 kg
* Speed: 2,5 m/s
* Environment: Indoor / Outdoor
* Enclosure class: IP53 weatherproof
* Autonomy: Up to 6 h
* Traction motors: 4 x 500 W with brake
* Kinematics: Omnidirectional - Dual Ackerman
* Localization system: SLAM
* Temperature range:  -10ºC to +45ºC
* Max. slope: 47%
