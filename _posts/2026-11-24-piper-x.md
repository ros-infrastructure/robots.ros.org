PiPER-X is a lightweight six-degree-of-freedom robotic arm developed by AgileX Robotics for embodied AI, robotic manipulation, robot learning and robotics education.

Unlike the standard PiPER, PiPER-X features a spherical wrist configuration with joint rotation ranges of ±90° for J4 and J5 and ±165° for J6. This wrist configuration provides flexible end-effector orientation for multi-angle grasping, insertion, alignment and manipulation in constrained workspaces.

The arm has a weight of 4.5 kg, a maximum payload of 1.5 kg, a reach of 669 mm and a repeatability of ±0.1 mm. It operates through a CAN communication interface with a 24 V DC power supply.

PiPER-X supports drag teaching, trajectory replay and PC-based control. Its public ROS 1 and ROS 2 drivers, Python SDK, URDF models and simulation resources enable developers to implement custom motion planning, robot control and manipulation workflows.

The lightweight design allows integration with desktop research setups, wheeled mobile robots and quadruped robots. PiPER-X can be configured with grippers, dexterous hands and vision sensors for mobile manipulation, teleoperation, imitation learning and sim-to-real research.
