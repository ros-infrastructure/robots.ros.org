TRACER 2.0 is a flat AGV base platform from AgileX Robotics, intended for in-plant material transport and as a base for custom AMR and intralogistics solutions.

With a height of only 169 mm the platform fits underneath transport structures, bin carriers or application-specific modules, and it carries a payload of up to 150 kg. The two-wheel differential drive lets it rotate on the spot, which keeps it manoeuvrable in aisles, at racking and between production stations. Two brushless 400 W motors give a top speed of up to 2.0 m/s.

TRACER 2.0 is an open base platform rather than a finished autonomous vehicle: navigation, safety sensors and the supervisory control layer are added according to the application. ROS and ROS 2 packages are published by the manufacturer.

Manufacturer: AgileX Robotics. Sales and support in the DACH region: PK Industrie GmbH, Aachen, Germany.
