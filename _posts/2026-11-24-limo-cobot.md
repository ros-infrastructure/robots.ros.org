LIMO COBOT is an integrated mobile manipulation platform developed by AgileX Robotics for robotics education, embodied AI research and mobile robotics development.

Based on the LIMO PRO mobile platform, it combines four interchangeable mobility configurations with a lightweight six-degree-of-freedom robotic arm. The arm supports a payload of 250 g, a working radius of 280 mm and a positioning precision of ±0.5 mm.

LIMO COBOT integrates NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano computing, an EAI T-mini Pro 2D LiDAR, an Orbbec Astra+ depth camera and an IMU. This hardware configuration supports SLAM, autonomous navigation, obstacle avoidance, object detection and pose estimation.

The ROS 2 development environment enables developers to combine mobile robot navigation with robotic arm control and vision-guided manipulation. Application scenarios include autonomous pick-and-place, object recognition, grasp planning and mobile manipulation experiments.

Educational resources include ROS 2 course materials, source code, simulation models and experiment manuals. An optional robotics training sand table supports structured experiments in navigation, perception and manipulation.
