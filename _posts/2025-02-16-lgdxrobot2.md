LGDXRobot2 is a DIY Mecanum wheel chassis for hobbyists with ROS2 integration. It is designed to be a low-cost, flexible platform that supports various sizes and hardware configurations. Moreover, LGDXRobot2 includes a GUI tool for hardware testing and fine-tuning PID settings for motors, enabling users to build high-performance robots with ease.

Last but not least, LGDXRobot2 integrates with ROS2 and an in-house cloud platform, facilitating the development of fully-fledged robotics applications.

The entire LGDXRobot2 project is publicly available under the MIT or GPLv3 licences. Below are the GitLab repositories.

* [Control Board Firmware](https://gitlab.com/yukaitung/lgdxrobot2-mcu)
* [Chassis Tuner](https://gitlab.com/yukaitung/lgdxrobot2-chassistuner)
* [ROS2 Package](https://gitlab.com/yukaitung/lgdxrobot2-ros2)
* [In-house Cloud Platform](https://gitlab.com/yukaitung/lgdxrobot2-cloud)
