BUNKER MINI 2.0 combines compact dimensions with the traction of a tracked chassis. AgileX Robotics designed it for inspection, measurement and research applications in confined areas as well as on uneven outdoor surfaces.

Two independently controlled 250 W motors drive the tracks and allow the platform to turn on the spot. A payload of up to 25 kg leaves room for sensors, computers, additional power supplies and lightweight manipulators.

IP67 protection, a CAN interface and a power output for external devices form the basis for custom robotic systems. ROS and ROS 2 packages are published by the manufacturer. A larger model, BUNKER PRO, is available for heavier payloads.

Manufacturer: AgileX Robotics. Sales and support in the DACH region: PK Industrie GmbH, Aachen, Germany.
